# Privacy Policy — PLANet2x creative studio

Planet2x Creative Studio respects your privacy.

Our games are designed to be calm, self-contained experiences. We do not sell user data, display ads, or track users for marketing purposes.

We only collect personal information if you voluntarily contact us by email or subscribe to our newsletter. This information is used solely for direct communication and is never shared or sold.

**Big and Little: Magic Journey** does not collect, store, or process any personal data.

There are no user accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking of any kind. The experience is fully offline and private by design.

**Liquid Path** collects limited, anonymous in-game event data to improve gameplay and stability.

These events do not identify you personally and are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

If you have questions about privacy or wish to request data deletion, you can contact us at

support@planet2x.com

Made with care by Planet2x Creative Studio.
