# Privacy Policy — Planet2x Creative Studio

Effective date: September 19, 2026

Planet2x games are created and published by Markku Anttila. Markku Anttila is the data controller for the processing described in this policy.

Contact: support@planet2x.com

## Lumen Path

Lumen Path does not require an account or login. It does not display advertising, use data for advertising personalization, sell personal data, or track users across apps or websites owned by other companies.

### Analytics

Lumen Path uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is used and to improve gameplay, stability, and store flows.

The analytics data may include:

- pseudonymous app-instance and device identifiers
- app launches, sessions, and product interactions
- gameplay events and Path usage
- App Store product, purchase-outcome, and entitlement events
- approximate country or region derived for analytics
- basic app, operating-system, and device information required by the analytics service

Lumen Path does not request precise GPS location. Analytics data is not used for advertising or cross-company tracking.

The legal basis for this processing is the controller’s legitimate interest in operating, understanding, securing, and improving Lumen Path.

### Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. Planet2x may process product identifiers, purchase outcomes, and entitlement status for app functionality and analytics. Planet2x does not receive your payment-card details or other payment credentials.

### Capture Still and Photos

Capture Still works only when you choose to use it. With your permission, Lumen Path saves the captured image locally to your Photos library. Planet2x does not upload the image.

### Local app data

Lumen Path stores settings, preferences, and game state locally on your device. This local data remains until it is changed, reset, or removed with the app, as applicable.

### Service providers

Google provides Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, and BigQuery services used for first-party analytics. Apple provides App Store, StoreKit, and Photos services. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies and may process data outside your country subject to their applicable safeguards.

### Retention

Google Analytics data is retained according to the configured Google Analytics service settings and Google’s applicable policies.

Raw Lumen Path analytics tables exported to Planet2x BigQuery storage expire 730 days after each table is created. The maximum intended retention for this raw exported analytics data is therefore 24 months.

Local app data remains on your device as described above.

### Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

Because analytics identifiers are pseudonymous and Lumen Path has no user accounts, Planet2x may need an app or device identifier to locate data associated with a request and may not always be able to connect analytics data to a named person.

To ask a privacy question or exercise your rights, contact:

support@planet2x.com

## Other Planet2x games

Big and Little: Magic Journey does not use accounts, analytics, advertising, or cross-app tracking.

Liquid Path processes limited pseudonymous in-game event data to improve gameplay and stability. It does not use this data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when Planet2x games, services, or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
